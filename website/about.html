About APEX Fitness

APEX Fitness was built with a clear purpose: complete sovereignty over your training data, zero corporate gatekeeping, and uncompromising engineering quality.

We believe lifters deserve a strength tracker that never holds their training hostage. Most commercial gym apps push subscriptions, lock basic analytics behind paywalls, and monetize personal fitness metrics. APEX Fitness takes the opposite approach: an offline-first, local-first architecture with optional cloud synchronization that you control.

Every feature in APEX is tested under rigorous real-world conditions: the guided workout flow, deterministic progression engines, warm-up calculators, rest timer alarms, and multi-perspective muscle activation maps (volume distributions, recovery balance, and fatigue modeling).

APEX Fitness is released under the AGPL-3.0 license. Your training logs belong to you, the code is fully auditable, and all third-party open-source contributions and exercise catalogue licensors are properly preserved and attributed.

Principles

  • Your data is yours. Local-first, complete JSON/ZIP backups, no lock-in.
  • No third-party trackers or telemetry. No advertising SDKs, no behavioral tracking cookies.
  • Deterministic & Auditable. Clean Node and React architectures with full test coverage across every feature.
  • Open source that stays open. AGPL-3.0 guarantees freedom for every user and contributor.
